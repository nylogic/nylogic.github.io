--- layout: talk title: Logic Workshop talk_title: The isomorphism relation for omnigenous locally finite groups categories: logic-workshop date: 2026-10-16 semester: fall-2026 speaker_first: Su speaker_last: Gao speaker_website: "https://logic.nankai.edu.cn/2022/0830/c29896a470117/pagem.htm" affiliation: Nankai University abstract: "

In 1959, P. Hall discovered a remarkable countable locally finite group $\mathbb{H}$ which is universal for all finite groups and has the ultrahomogeneity property. He showed that $\mathbb{H}$ is the unique group with these properties. An omnigenous locally finite group is defined by a weaker form of the ultrahomogeneity property. Nevertheless, we show that the class of all countable omnigenous locally finite groups has the maximum Borel cardinality of isomorphism types among all countable structures. This is joint work with Feng Li.
